Common Questions

FAQ

Things people usually ask before reaching out.

How quickly do you respond?

I aim to reply to every message within 24 hours on weekdays. For urgent requests, WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach me.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, absolutely. If your project requires confidentiality I'm happy to sign an NDA before any technical discussion takes place.

What does a typical project cost?

Small automation scripts start around $100–300. Full Django applications typically fall in the $500–3,000 range depending on scope. Complex SaaS platforms and AI systems are quoted individually.

How many revisions do I get?

Revisions are scoped per project. Small fixes and adjustments within the agreed deliverables are included. Larger scope changes are quoted separately and agreed upfront.

Which payment methods do you accept?

I accept payments via Wise, PayPal, Payoneer, and bank transfer. For freelance platform clients, Fiverr and Upwork are also supported.

Can you work with my existing codebase?

Yes. I regularly take on projects involving existing Django codebases — whether that's adding features, fixing bugs, or refactoring legacy code for scale.
