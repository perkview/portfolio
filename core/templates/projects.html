The Journey

Development Timeline

How the work has evolved — from first projects to production systems.

2022

Foundation — Python & Django Basics

Started with Python fundamentals, moved into Django, built the first CRUD applications, learned database design, and deployed on shared hosting. First real project: a personal blog with custom admin.

2023

Scale-Up — Full Stack Systems

Expanded into multi-app Django projects, REST APIs with DRF, Stripe payment integration, role-based auth, and async task queues. Built the Video Platform and QuantumTask during this period.

2024

AI Integration & Automation Systems

Integrated LLMs via OpenRouter and LangChain, built the self-hosted AI chatbot with voice I/O, designed the trading bot system, and began taking on client freelance projects requiring AI components.

2025 — Present

Client Work & Production Deployments

Focused on freelance client delivery — scoped requirements, built proposals, shipped production systems with proper documentation. Deployed on VPS infrastructure with CI-ready codebases.
